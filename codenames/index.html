How to play (in person, same table)

  • Split into two teams: Red and Blue. Each team picks one Spymaster; everyone else is a Guesser.
  • This device is shared. Set it in the middle so guessers can see the board.
  • At the start of each turn, only the spymasters should look at the screen while one of them taps "Reveal Key (Spymasters Only)" to see which words belong to which team. Guessers should look away.
  • The spymaster gives a one-word clue plus a number, e.g. "OCEAN — 2", meaning two words on the board relate to "ocean". Type it into the clue box so everyone can see it, or just say it out loud.
  • Guessers tap words they think match the clue. Tap "Hide Key" first so nobody accidentally sees the answers!
  • Guessing your own team's color lets you keep guessing. Guessing neutral (tan) or the other team's color ends your turn. Guessing the black Assassin card loses the game instantly for your team.
  • First team to reveal all of their own words wins.
